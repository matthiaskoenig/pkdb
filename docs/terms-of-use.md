# PK-DB Terms of Use

Last revised: 24 September 2026.

These terms govern access to and reuse of the curated PK-DB database and its online data services. The [PK-DB source code](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkdb/blob/develop/LICENSE) and documentation retain their separate software and documentation licenses; those licenses do not grant a license to the curated database.

## Attribution and citation

All outputs that use PK-DB data, including publications, preprints, reports, presentations, datasets, services, and products, must acknowledge PK-DB and cite the original PK-DB paper:

> Grzegorzewski J, Brandhorst J, Green K, Eleftheriadou D, Duport Y, Barthorscht F, Köller A, Ke DYJ, De Angelis S, König M. *PK-DB: pharmacokinetics database for individualized and stratified computational modeling*. Nucleic Acids Research. 2021;49(D1):D1358–D1364. [doi:10.1093/nar/gkaa990](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaa990). [PubMed: 33151297](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33151297/).

You must also cite the original publications for the studies whose data you use. Preserve the study identifiers and source references supplied by PK-DB so that readers can trace reused data to the original evidence. Include links to the PK-DB paper, the database at [alpha.pk-db.com](https://alpha.pk-db.com), and the original study publications wherever the output format supports links. See [Citing PK-DB](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/pkdb/citation/) for citation details and the software citation.

## Substantial reuse and coauthorship

Use of data from **more than 10 distinct PK-DB studies (11 or more)** in a project constitutes substantial reuse. Count distinct studies across the project, including data used in analyses, model development, or validation; splitting the data across downloads does not change the threshold.

For publications arising from substantial reuse, **Matthias König, representing the Systems Medicine of the Liver group, must be included as a coauthor** in recognition of the substantial scientific work invested in curating and maintaining the data. This requirement is additional to citation of the PK-DB paper and the original study publications.

Contact [Matthias König](mailto:koenigmx@hu-berlin.de) before undertaking substantial reuse to arrange the collaboration, scientific contributions, manuscript review, and approval. Authorship must meet the applicable journal's requirements, including contribution, approval, and accountability criteria such as the [ICMJE recommendations](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/roles-and-responsibilities/defining-the-role-of-authors-and-contributors.html). Do not list anyone as an author without their agreement. If these requirements cannot be met, contact Matthias König to resolve the conditions before proceeding with substantial reuse.

## Commercial use and data licensing

**Commercial use of the curated PK-DB database requires a separate data license agreed in writing before use.** Commercial use is available through licensing; contact [Matthias König](mailto:koenigmx@hu-berlin.de) at the [Systems Medicine of the Liver group](https://livermetabolism.com) to discuss the intended use, scope, and licensing terms. Access through the website, downloads, the API, or the Python client does not itself grant a commercial data license.

Attribution and citation remain required for licensed commercial use unless the written license expressly provides otherwise. Any applicable third-party rights in original study publications or contributed data remain in force; a PK-DB data license does not grant rights held by third parties.
