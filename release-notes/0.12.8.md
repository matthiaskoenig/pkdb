# Release notes for pkdb 0.12.8

pkdb 0.12.8 adds the vocabulary measurement `disease duration`, which format 1 studies that kept the duration of a disease in the numbers of their `disease` choice row need for their conversion to study format 2. The database needs no migration; the server applies the new vocabulary at startup.

## Changes

- The section tabs of a study in the local curation app follow the order of the work on a study: Metadata, Data, Problems, Review and Activity, also in the select of a narrow window. A study still opens on Review when it has open items, on Problems when it has errors, and on Metadata otherwise (#961).

- The vocabulary has the measurement `disease duration` (years since the diagnosis or the onset of a disease), because a choice row of `disease` holds no numbers in study format 2 (#986). It encodes the diabetes duration of format 1 studies that kept it in the mean of their `disease` row, such as glucose/Basu2009, and the description of `disease` points to it.
