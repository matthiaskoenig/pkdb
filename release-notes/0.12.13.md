# Release notes for pkdb 0.12.13

pkdb 0.12.13 closes the last gap of the conversion of study format 1 folders: `pkdb migrate` refuses a study when a row that it converts holds a number in a column that `study.json` does not map, as it already did for rows that it drops, so no number of a converted row is lost without notice, and the vocabulary has the measurement `radioactivity excretion rate`. An audit of pkdb_data found such losses in 300 converted studies, which pkdb_data#2741 restored; every study of pkdb_data is in study format 2 now. The database needs no migration.

## Changes

- The vocabulary has the measurement `radioactivity excretion rate` (`Bq/min`, `cpm/min`), the radioactivity of a tracer excreted per time, such as the urinary excretion of label after a 14C-labelled drug (#1018): hydrochlorothiazide/Beermann1976 Fig. 2 reports it in cpm x 10^3 x min^-1. A count rate does not convert to an activity.

- `pkdb migrate` refuses a study with `unmapped_value` when a format 1 row that it converts holds a number in a column that `study.json` does not map, not only when it drops the row (#1015): the converter writes only the mapped columns, so the conversion lost the min, max or range next to a mapped median or mean, cv columns, digitized error bars whose spread is not mapped, baselines and changes, quartiles, the other dimension of a scatter and whole measurements that no template reads. An audit of the converted studies of pkdb_data found about 7,200 such numbers in 280 studies. The difference names the format 1 file, sheet and row, the subjects and measurements of its records and the columns with their numbers, such as `unmapped_value Frost2014.xlsx Fig2 row 3, subject all, concentration` with `value 16.87`. A number that a kept record of the row holds is no loss: a mapped column; a cell that a formula computes only from held cells and counts or times, in any row of the sheet (a change computed from the mean and the mean of the baseline row); the input that the formula of a held cell determines, the only unknown number that it reads as a single cell of its row with arithmetic, text joins, `ABS`, `SQRT`, `EXP`, `LN`, `LOG` or `LOG10` (the `mean + se` column of `ABS(X - mean)`, the cv of `=cv/100*mean`, the index in a name); and an exact copy of a statistic of the record or of the end of its error bar (a `value` column that repeats the mean, a `mean_pm` column that is the mean plus the typed sd). An aggregate determines none of its inputs, so the check now also refuses the individual values behind a mapped average (torasemide/Lesne1982), the confidence limits, ranges and quartiles from which an sd is computed, and a baseline and change from which a mean is computed; until now any cell that a formula of a mapped cell read counted as kept. The columns `individual_count`, `subjects`, `day`, `tstart`, `tend`, `t_start`, `t_end`, `traw` and every column whose name starts with `time` hold no value. On the format 1 states of the 1448 converted studies the gate refuses 484 studies, where it refused 127 before: 429 lose numbers that the audit or later restores confirm, 24 numbers behind aggregates of mapped cells, 6 have error bar formulas that read the neighbouring row, and 25 only helpers such as error bar ends next to a rounded spread or a logarithm column.
