# Release notes for pkdb 0.12.5

pkdb 0.12.5 converts the counts that the format 1 tables hold beside the count of their group, which the converted studies of pkdb_data lacked, and makes the warning `inconsistent_statistics` name the count with which the values agree. The local curation app numbers the lines of a table as curators count them, shows the problems of a cell in a tooltip, keeps up with 1,000 studies, shows the unsaved changes of `study.json` in a banner and saves them with Ctrl+S, opens the workbook at the sheet and cell of a problem, filters the studies by every substance their tables name and switches the axes of its plot between linear and log. Continuous integration runs its tests in parallel jobs. The database needs no migration.

## Changes

- `pkdb migrate` writes the count of an output or timecourse point of a group from the `count` column of its format 1 table when it differs from the count of the group, as the intended change `table_count` (#955). No format 1 template maps `count`, because the format 1 server counted every output with its group, but curators kept the number of subjects of a row in that column and derived the sd or se of the row from it, so the converted studies warned `inconsistent_statistics`, such as enalapril/Wagner2003. A count above the count of the group, or one that is no whole number, is not written and is listed under Manual decisions of `migration.md`; an individual keeps the count 1. The converted studies of pkdb_data got these counts in pkdb_data#2497.

- The plot of the mapped rows in the Data section of the local curation app switches each axis between a linear and a log scale (#945). The scale of the WebPlotDigitizer project of the figure is the default, the app keeps the choice per figure with its settings, and a note says how many points at 0 or below a log axis leaves out.

- The warning `inconsistent_statistics` names the count that a standard error needs and whose count it is, for example `error_bar 0.5452 (se) with count 9 of the group enalapril implies sd 0.4934`, adds the count with which the two values agree when it is the only whole count that fits the reported digits (`; they agree with count 5`), and shows its numbers with 4 significant digits (#944). Its context holds `count`, `count_source` and `agreeing_count`.

- The Substance filter of the local curation app also shows the studies whose interventions or data name the substance, by the rule of the substance labels of the study issues, such as aliskiren/Nussberger2002 under enalapril (#946). The tooltip of the study name lists these other substances.

- The local curation app opens the workbook at the right sheet: Open sheet in the Data section at the sheet of the shown table, and Open in tables of a problem at the sheet and the cell of the problem (#948). A workbook that is open already opens as it is, and a notice names the sheet and the cell to go to.

- The local curation app shows the problems of an outlined cell, header cell or line number of a table in a tooltip on hover after 300 ms, and every problem of a row when the keyboard focuses its line number, with severity, code, message and hint (#951). The plot of the mapped rows of a figure rings the points of rows with problems and lists the problems in their hover label.

- The paper viewer of the local curation app no longer logs an error of PDF.js to the console of the browser when a PDF closes while the viewer still opens it, as when another section is chosen quickly (#951).

- Tables number their lines as curators count them (#949). In a table with a header, such as `outputs_Fig6.tsv`, the header is line 0 and the first entry line 1 in the curation app (the rows, the problems and the address of the Data section), in validation messages, in conflicts of `pkdb tables sync` and on the command line, and `pkdb review acknowledge --line` takes these lines; a raw table still starts at line 1. A location names the line and the address of the cell in the workbook, such as `line 96 · error_bar · workbook cell Y97` in the app and `outputs_Fig6.tsv → line 96 → error_bar → workbook cell Y97` in `pkdb validate`, and problems of the workbook itself keep the rows of its sheets. Reports of earlier validations show the old numbers until the study is validated again, which `pkdb curate` does when it starts.

- The local curation app shows the state of `study.json` in a banner under the section tabs of Metadata and Data, which replaces the save bar at the bottom (#952). It says that there are no unsaved changes, turns amber and names the changed fields when there are, and keeps its height, so that the form does not move; it stays in view while the page scrolls as long as it has something to say, and the other sections show it from their opening while there are unsaved changes, a notice or a problem of the last save, until the curator leaves them. Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on macOS) saves them from anywhere on the study page, also a collaborator that is typed but not yet added. The header of a study wraps onto more lines instead of cutting the text of its chips, and shows what the study is doing, such as Validating for a moment after a save, beside its title, so that the header keeps its height, and in a narrow window the source pane of the Data section fits a short image instead of leaving empty space below it.

- The overview of the local curation app keeps up with typing in **Search studies** in a workspace of 1,000 studies (#953). It filters 150 ms after the last keystroke and renders only the rows in view, the local server answers an unchanged state without building it, and typing in the Metadata form redraws only the changed field. The Substance and Curator lists sort numerically and ignore case, and the rows of `/local/state` no longer carry `problems`, which the study detail does.

- The open actions of the local curation app and `pkdb tables open` no longer fail after 15 seconds while the program they started, such as LibreOffice, runs (#943). The app starts the open command without waiting for the program that it starts: a command that fails within 2 seconds shows its error as before, and a later failure goes to the log file and the terminal of `pkdb curate`.

- Continuous integration runs its test jobs in parallel and skips them for a pull request that changes only the documentation (#950). The frontend browser tests run one job per browser and the curation app browser tests on every core, the jobs that start the backend image build it from a layer cache, every backend test copies a template database that is migrated once instead of migrating its own, the test database runs without durable writes, and the five speed tests of the client run apart with `pytest -m slow`.

- The paper viewer of the local curation app no longer logs two errors of PDF.js to the console of the browser when a PDF closes while a page of it loads, as when another PDF is chosen quickly (#930).
