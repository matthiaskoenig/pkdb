# Unreleased

- The vocabulary has the substances, tissues, methods and choices that format 1 studies need for their conversion to study format 2: the substances `naltrexone` (morphine/Dayno2017), `mycophenolate mofetil` and `prednisone` (rapamycin/Rogers2008), `bupropion hydrochloride` (chlorzoxazone/Puris2019), `black grape juice` (omeprazole/Iftikhar2024), the protein `CYP2E1` (chlorzoxazone/Mishin1998) and the transporters `ABCB1`, `ABCC2`, `ABCC3`, `ABCG2`, `SLC10A2`, `SLC15A1`, `SLC22A1`, `SLC22A3` and `SLCO2B1` (named by their genes, with the protein names such as P-gp, BCRP, ASBT, PEPT1, OCT1 and OATP2B1 as synonyms, talinolol/Drozdzik2014); the tissues `duodenum`, `jejunum`, `ileum`, `colon`, `ascending colon`, `transverse colon`, `descending colon` and `sigmoid colon`; the methods `Jaffe reaction`, `Jaffe reaction (AutoAnalyzer)` and `Jaffe reaction (Lloyd reagent)` (the regular, the AutoAnalyzer and the true creatinine of creatinine/Bauer1982), `qRT-PCR`, `western blot`, `intra-arterial catheter`, `pulmonary artery catheter` and `thermodilution` (ramipril/deGraeff1989); the ethnicity `other` (semaglutide/Buckley2018), the disease `hypertriglyceridemia` and the medical procedure `lipoprotein apheresis` (pravastatin/Raasch1991).

- The vocabulary has the measurements that format 1 studies need for their conversion to study format 2: `urinary volume rate (change)`, `excretion rate (change)` and `osmolarity (change)` (changes from baseline per collection interval, as in canagliflozin/Iijima2015), `auc_net_incremental` (the net area of a curve above minus below its baseline, such as the decremental AUC of blood glucose, time required) and `concentration change absolute maximum` and `concentration change absolute minimum` (the maximum increase and decrease from baseline, glimepiride/Niemi2002), `radioactivity concentration` (Bq/l or a count rate in cpm/ml) and `arteriovenous concentration difference` (inulin/Cousins1999), `mRNA expression` (relative to a reference gene of the same sample) and `protein amount` (pmol/mg protein by targeted proteomics or arbitrary units per mg protein by western blot, talinolol/Drozdzik2014 and chlorzoxazone/Mishin1998), and `filtered load` (such as the filtered glucose, empagliflozin/Sarashina2014). All changes, the net incremental AUC and the arteriovenous difference may be negative. The unit `cpm` (counts per minute) has its own dimension, so a count rate never converts to an activity in Bq.

- The web interface guide describes the Studies page and the study page with what each reader sees, and the local curation guide notes that the website shows a study with the same views; all screenshots of both guides are rendered again, the website ones with the new `tools/website_docs/run.sh` on the disposable website of the browser tests.
- A concentration timecourse that is all zero, such as the concentrations of a placebo or below the limit of quantification, has no PK parameters, where before its study failed with `pk_zero_curve`. Three format 1 studies of pkdb_data that the conversion refused for it now convert.
- `pkdb migrate` no longer refuses a dropped row with `unmapped_value` for numbers that another record of the same row keeps through a formula of the workbook: a column that the formula of a mapped cell of that record reads, such as the `mean + se` column of an error bar `ABS(X - mean)`, and a column computed only from these and the count, such as an sd from that se. The C-peptide record of a format 1 row whose glucose was not sampled (`glucose/LicinioPaixao1986`) holds them, so the study converts.
