# Unreleased

- The section tabs of a study in the local curation app follow the order of the work on a study: Metadata, Data, Problems, Review and Activity, also in the select of a narrow window. A study still opens on Review when it has open items, on Problems when it has errors, and on Metadata otherwise (#961).
- The plot of mapped rows in the Data section of the local curation app, the overlay on the image and `pkdb plot` draw the error bar of each timecourse row that has a statistic: the first one that fits, the statistic that `error_type` names first, then around a `gmean` the `gsd`, `sd` and `se`, and around a `mean` or `median` the `sd` and `se`, as the value minus and plus it or divided and multiplied by a `gsd`; a digitized `error_bar` gives the named statistic when its column is empty, a negative `sd` or `se` or a `gsd` below 1 is skipped and the next statistic is tried, a row with no valid statistic has no bar, and a log axis cuts a bar that reaches 0 or below at the axis (#958).
- The plot of mapped rows in the Data section of the local curation app and `pkdb plot` draw the rows of `outputs_<source>.tsv` of a figure that have a `time` like timecourses: one series of lines and markers by time per subjects, interventions, measurement, substance and tissue, such as `all · D150 · concentration · caffeine · plasma`, beside the image or, with a WebPlotDigitizer dataset of that name, on it. A click on a point selects its row, and a point whose row has a problem has a ring. Validation still compares only timecourse and scatter rows with a project, and a figure without rows to plot says **No rows with a time or scatter rows to plot.** `pkdb plot` plots such figures by default (#942).
