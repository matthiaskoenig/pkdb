# Unreleased

- The web interface guide describes the Studies page and the study page with what each reader sees, and the local curation guide notes that the website shows a study with the same views; all screenshots of both guides are rendered again, the website ones with the new `tools/website_docs/run.sh` on the disposable website of the browser tests.
- The plot of mapped rows of the local curation app and of the website, and `pkdb plot`, draw the rows of `outputs_<source>.tsv` with a `time` by time only when time is the x axis of the figure: when the figure has timecourse rows or an outputs series at two or more times. A figure whose every outputs series has one time, such as the change of HbA1c at the end of the treatment per exposure quartile of liraglutide/Ingwersen2012 Fig2, no longer has its rows drawn at the time on an axis of concentration or dose; its digitized points stay on the image, and `pkdb plot` no longer plots such a figure by default. A figure without rows to plot says **No rows over time or scatter rows to plot.** The stored study pages are built again (`VIEW_VERSION` 2, `pkdb-server rebuild-views`).
