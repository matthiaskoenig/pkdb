# Unreleased

- The local curation app says what failed and why (#923). Every failed request of the local curation server answers with a message that names the action and the study, the detail of its cause (for a program that could not open a file: its name, its exit status and the end of its error output), the action, a request id and the time, and an unexpected error adds its traceback. The app shows the message and the detail in its alerts, with Details (the facts and the traceback), Copy details, which copies them as Markdown for an issue, and Show in Activity; a study, table or source that cannot be read says why. Failed changes of a study that the server logs are listed in its Activity, a failed job keeps its error, and File watching shows Error while the file watcher fails. The server writes these failures with their traceback to `logs/curation.log` in its state directory (by default `~/.cache/pkdb/curation/` on Linux) and one line naming the request id to the terminal of `pkdb curate`; a failure that repeats is logged once a minute, and the API key and the tokens appear in no answer, log or terminal line. `pkdb tables open` says which command failed instead of printing a traceback.

- The Data section of the local curation app shows the tables first and color codes them (#924). The navigator lists the tables under Subjects, Interventions, Data and Raw extractions, each with a bar in the color of its class of information (blue, green, violet and gray), its problems, open review items and rows, and the sources follow below, in a navigator as wide as its longest name up to 320 pixels; the rows pane tints its table in the same color and frames the selected row. The subjects, characteristica and interventions tables show every row with a Source select in the head of the rows pane (All, each source, No source) in place of the switch Rows of the source only, choosing a table selects its source, and the section opens on the first table with problems or open review items. The source pane fades out at its bottom while it scrolls, and the thumbnails of large paper images no longer stay white in the navigator.

- The Data section of the local curation app shows the notes of a table above the table (#926). A line such as Notes of the subjects says what study.json notes about that kind of table, opens to edit its descriptions and comments, opens by itself when a field in it has a problem, and shows the same notes for every table of the kind. The Metadata and Data sections edit one draft of study.json with one bar to save, discard and undo, the tab of each section marks its unsaved fields, switching between the sections keeps the edits, and leaving the study with unsaved changes asks first. The card for the notes of each table is gone from Metadata, which keeps only the notes of a kind that the study has no table of, and Show notes in Problems opens the notes of a table for a problem in them.

- The local curation app shows the PDF of a study in the Data section (#917). Paper is the first source: the PDF of the study and the other PDFs at the top of its folder are drawn inside the app with PDF.js, with continuous pages, selectable text, zoom, fit width and fit page, page controls and find (Enter and Shift+Enter, Ctrl+F in the pane); the head of the pane has a select of the PDFs, their size, New tab, which opens the PDF in a tab of its own without the app bar, and Open in PDF program. The address names the chosen PDF as `pdf`, the viewer keeps its page and zoom while other sources show and opens a PDF again on its page when it is saved anew, a large PDF shows its first page without being read whole, and the Content Security Policy is unchanged. The local server streams images and PDFs in pieces, answers byte ranges and serves PDFs inline, the study page lists its PDFs as `papers` with their size and a version of their content, and a file that changes and changes back before the next scan of the folder shows again.
