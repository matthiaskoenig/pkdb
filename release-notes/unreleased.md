# Unreleased

## Changes

- Security: the frontend overrides minimatch with 11.0.4, which fixes GHSA-g3hv-75wg-m249 (patched in 11.0.1, no fixed 10.x release), and glob with 14.0.3, the glob built against minimatch 11, in `overrides` of `package.json`. The newest releases of eslint (10.14.0), typescript-eslint (8.72.1) and editorconfig (3.0.2) still require minimatch 10. Only development tools use them: eslint, typescript-eslint, and js-beautify of `@vue/test-utils`. The overrides can be removed once they accept minimatch 11 (#1057).
- `pkdb plot` draws its figures as charts of sbmlsim-core (`sbmlsim_core.chart`) instead of matplotlib and writes them as SVG, `<out>/<study>_<source>.plot.svg`, with the figure image embedded: the image with the digitized points, the mapped rows and their error bars and the legends of the series and of the marks below it, or the image beside the plot of the data. `pkdb.studyformat.plot.render_source` writes SVG, or PNG for a path ending in `.png`, and its `PlotResult` carries the drawn `chart`. The `pkdb` package depends on sbmlsim-core 0.34.0 or newer instead of matplotlib; `tools/curation_testing/make_fixture.py`, which draws the scanned figure and the PDFs of the test fixture, runs with `--with matplotlib` (#1059).
