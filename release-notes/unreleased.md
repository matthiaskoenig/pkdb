# Unreleased

- The views of a study in the local curation app, its study list, header, section tabs and its Data, Problems and Review sections, live in `frontend/src/study-views/` for the website to use too, behind one context of what the reader may see and do, the routes of the page and its reads; the curation app looks and works as before, except that its duplicate studies, which show - for their problems, now sort last by Problems (#963).
- On save of the local curation app is one setting for the workspace, the server and the PK-DB account, in a menu of the top bar beside File watching, instead of an action per study (#966). The menu lists Validate, Upload and Off with what a save then does; Upload asks to confirm first, and offline or without an account that may upload it is disabled and says why. The overview loses the On save column and the On save select of its batch toolbar, and the tab line of a study loses On save. `GET /local/state` has `mode` once instead of in every study row, and `POST /local/mode` takes only `mode`. The actions per study that pkdb 0.12 saved are not taken over: On save starts at Validate, and pkdb 0.12 keeps reading its own.

- The overview of the local curation app shows the release, the issue and the curators of a study right after the study, before its review status, open items, problems and sync status, and puts the search, the Substance and Curator filters and the status chips on one line with the heading and the number of studies; in a narrower window the status chips move to a second line first (#964, #965).

- The Data section of the local curation app tints the columns of a table by what they name: the subjects blue, the interventions green and the other data gray, with `study` and `source` in muted italics without a tint. `subjects.tsv` and `characteristica.tsv` stay blue and `interventions.tsv` green, raw extractions are no longer tinted, and the navigator marks the tables of data with a gray bar instead of a violet one and the raw extractions with an outlined bar (#959).

- A name of a subject or an intervention in a table of the Data section of the local curation app opens its row in `subjects.tsv` or `interventions.tsv`, selected and scrolled into view, and browser Back returns to the row it came from (#960). The names of the `subjects`, `parent` and `interventions` columns and of the columns that end in `_subjects` or `_interventions` are links, each name of a cell of several names its own; the Right and Left arrow keys reach them from the line number of the row, and Enter follows one.

- The plot of mapped rows in the Data section of the local curation app, the overlay on the image and `pkdb plot` draw the error bar of each timecourse row that has a statistic: the first one that fits, the statistic that `error_type` names first, then around a `gmean` the `gsd`, `sd` and `se`, and around a `mean` or `median` the `sd` and `se`, as the value minus and plus it or divided and multiplied by a `gsd`; a digitized `error_bar` gives the named statistic when its column is empty, a negative `sd` or `se` or a `gsd` below 1 is skipped and the next statistic is tried, a row with no valid statistic has no bar, and a log axis cuts a bar that reaches 0 or below at the axis (#958).

- The plot of mapped rows in the Data section of the local curation app and `pkdb plot` draw the rows of `outputs_<source>.tsv` of a figure that have a `time` like timecourses: one series of lines and markers by time per subjects, interventions, measurement, substance and tissue, such as `all · D150 · concentration · caffeine · plasma`, beside the image or, with a WebPlotDigitizer dataset of that name, on it. A click on a point selects its row, and a point whose row has a problem has a ring. Validation still compares only timecourse and scatter rows with a project, and a figure without rows to plot says **No rows with a time or scatter rows to plot.** `pkdb plot` plots such figures by default (#942).

- The backend stores the documents of the study page of each study format 2 study at upload and serves them by audience at `/api/v2/studies/{substance}/{name}/view` and `/api/v2/study-rows`, gzip-compressed with weak ETags, built by the same library functions as the local curation app (`pkdb.studyformat.views`); who may write a study gets its curation view, everyone else a public view without review, acknowledged warnings, review targets, problems, issue, curator ratings, provenance details, raw tables, images and PDFs. A page that cannot be built does not fail the upload, and `pkdb-server rebuild-views` builds missing and older stored pages again. Readers who may not write a study no longer get its review, issue, review status and open review items from the JSON API, nor its `review.json` from the lists of files and `/media`; the JSON API keeps the curator ratings, which released `pkdb` clients require (#963).

- The local curation app no longer reports the error `Transport destroyed` in the browser console when a study section with a PDF closes while the PDF still loads its first page, such as on a quick switch from Data to Metadata: PDF.js rejects a promise of its viewer that nothing waits for, and the app now handles it.

- The overview of the local curation app spends about a third less time on each poll of the state while jobs run: a poll keeps the objects of the studies and jobs that did not change, so only the rows of the studies that changed render again and the widths of the columns are measured only for them. With 1,000 studies, a poll no longer renders every shown row and goes through every study for the column widths.
- The frontend of the Docker development stack starts again after the Docker daemon restarts, as the backend and the database do, and a stop no longer logs npm errors.
