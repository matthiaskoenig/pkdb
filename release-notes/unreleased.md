# Unreleased

- `pkdb migrate` refuses to drop a format 1 row without a value when any column that `study.json` does not map holds a number (`unmapped_value`), except counts, times, names and comments. The check knew only the statistic columns such as `mean`, `median` or `gmean`, so rows with their numbers only in columns such as `geomean`, `geometric_mean`, `ci_low`, `ci_high`, `q25` or `q75` were dropped unseen; an audit of the converted studies of pkdb_data found 59 such rows in 6 studies.

- The vocabulary allows values below zero for `ACE inhibition`, `factor Xa inhibition`, `factor Xa inhibition maximum` and `pressor response inhibition`: an inhibition is measured against the activity before the drug, and assay noise or a digitized curve at the baseline gives small negative values, which format 1 studies such as ramipril/Bainbridge1991 and rivaroxaban/Kubitza2005 hold. The new measurements `csf/plasma ratio` (the cerebrospinal fluid concentration over the plasma or serum concentration, as in morphine/Dale2007) and `renal plasma flow` (RPF or ERPF, as in hydrochlorothiazide/Dussol2005) close two vocabulary gaps of format 1 studies. The vocabulary also has the substances `nitrendipine`, `dihydralazine`, `clonidine`, `prazosin`, `methyldopa` (ramipril/Schunkert1989a), `mycophenolic acid` and `mycophenolic acid glucuronide` (MPAG, rapamycin/Rogers2008), `salbutamol 4-O-sulphate` (the sulphate conjugate, not the salt `salbutamol sulphate`) and `salbutamol-metabolites` (salbutamol and its sulphate conjugate, albuterol/Hindle1992), and the disease `IgA nephropathy`.

- The local curation app calls itself Curation in its app bar and its browser tab, and its app bar is teal with white text: a warning or an error in the badges of File watching and Connection shows in amber or red with dark text, and every text and badge of the bar keeps the contrast of WCAG AA in the light and the dark theme, also when hovered or focused. The header and the tabs of a study stand on a teal-tinted band, which sets them apart from the white cards of the section below (#967, #962).

- The descriptions and comments of a study and of the notes of its tables in the local curation app no longer show a label such as Description 1 or Comment 1 by curator: each description and comment starts one line high, as high as the other fields, and grows with its text, and a comment shows its author once above its text, and screen readers still hear the name of each field (#954).

- On Windows, `pkdb upload --report` and `--resume` no longer stop a batch when another program has the report open for a moment while it is rewritten, such as a reader of the report, a virus scanner or a search indexer: Windows refuses to replace an open file, so the report write now retries for up to 5 seconds before the batch stops with a report error.
