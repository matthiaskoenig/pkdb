# Unreleased

- The vocabulary has the pharmacodynamic summary measurements `ACE inhibition maximum`, `ACE inhibition tmax` and `ACE inhibition AUEC`, and `renin activity (change absolute) maximum`, `renin activity (change absolute) tmax` and `renin activity (change absolute) AUEC`: the maximal effect, the time of the maximal effect and the area under the effect curve, which hydrochlorothiazide/Giudicelli1987 Table 5 reports for the plasma converting enzyme inhibition and the plasma renin activity increase after captopril and hydrochlorothiazide. An AUEC also takes `arbitrary_unit`, which converts to no physical unit. The kidney function test `filtration fraction` (FF, the glomerular filtration rate divided by the renal plasma flow) is new as well (hydrochlorothiazide/Dussol2005 Table 1).
