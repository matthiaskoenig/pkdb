# Unreleased

- Convert with the vocabulary of the pkdb_data checkout in `pkdb migrate` (#883). Like `pkdb check`, it uses `--vocabulary FILE`, else `vocabulary.lock.json` of the checkout, else the vocabulary bundled with pkdb, so CI checks converted studies with the vocabulary that converted them.

- Convert more format 1 studies with `pkdb migrate` without curation (#882). Characteristica of measurements that need a time, such as concentrations, get the time `NR`, because format 1 characteristica have no time. Characteristica, outputs and timecourse points without any value, that is without a choice and without any statistic, are dropped instead of failing format 2 validation; rows with only a count, an error type or a spread, rows of `abstinence ...` and `fasting (duration)`, whose existence is the information, and repeats with data still need a curator. `migration.json` lists each dropped row with its table, its format 1 file, sheet and row, substance, tissue and comment, and `migration.md` lists them per sheet with row ranges for the studies that are written. The equivalence gate checks that every dropped row is empty and counts the changes as `time_not_reported` and `valueless_row`, and text that the converter writes on one line, such as a unit with two spaces or a blank substance cell, as `whitespace` instead of a mismatch. Both report files name the vocabulary of the run and of each study, and warn as long as they hold written studies of another vocabulary than the run's.

- Check the study format 2 studies of a pkdb_data checkout with `pkdb check` (#881), for its pre-commit hook and CI. `pkdb check [PATH...] [--staged | --changed BASE]` checks the studies touched by staged files or since `BASE`, or all studies, for canonical form and offline validation errors, refuses a tracked workbook, runs the checks of `pkdb registry --check` over the studies git tracks, skips format 1 and deleted studies, never writes and never contacts the network, and prints human or JSON output with exit code 0, 1 for problems or 2 for usage errors. Inside a pkdb_data checkout every offline validation, also in the curation app, now uses `--vocabulary`, then `vocabulary.lock.json` of the checkout, then the cached vocabulary of the endpoint, then the bundled one; `pkdb check` never uses the cache, and a broken lock is an error.

- Create, move, release and list study format 2 studies (#880). `pkdb new <substance>/<name> (--pmid N | --doi D) --licence ... --access private` writes a draft study with `study.json`, `reference.json`, `subjects.tsv` and `review.json`, takes the PDF and images from `papers/`, and creates or adopts its GitHub issue; with `--agent` it records an automatic curation. `pkdb move` renames a study with its files, review targets, provenance assets and issue title, and refuses a workbook with edits that are not in the tables. `pkdb release` gives approved studies the next PKDB identifiers, all or nothing, and `--access public` publishes them with their release. `pkdb registry` lists released studies and `--check` fails on a shared identifier or issue number. An upload of a moved study keeps its stored row through its issue number when it is the same publication and source, issue numbers are unique among studies (migration `p007issueunique`), and a manual and an automatic curation of one paper can be uploaded together.

- Keep one GitHub issue per study format 2 study with `pkdb issues sync` (#879). It aligns the title `<substance>/<name>`, the substance label and one workflow label (`curate`, `check` or `approved`), the curators and reviewers as assignees and the open or closed state, leaves other labels and issues without a study alone, and shows only the plan with `--dry-run`. `--adopt --user <user>` gives studies without an issue their existing issue by title, also with the prefixes `Curate `, `Check ` and `Check and curate `, closes open duplicates as not planned, or creates one, and records the number in `study.json`; an interrupted run is finished by the next one. The server lists administrators, curators and reviewers with their GitHub logins at `GET /api/v2/curators`, read by `Client.curators()`, and the curation app reads GitHub through the same client, now also with `GITHUB_TOKEN`.

- Convert format 1 studies to study format 2 with `pkdb migrate` (#878). Each study is converted in a work folder and proven by an equivalence gate that prepares the format 1 and the format 2 study and compares their records, comments, subjects, timecourses and scatters after the named intended changes; only identical studies and studies with intended changes replace their format 1 folder, all others stay format 1 with the reason in `migration.md` and `migration.json`. Folders without `study.json` move to `papers/<substance>/<name>/`, registered studies become approved with the release of `study_identifiers.json`, and a format 1 PKDB identifier that the registry does not give the study is refused. `--dry-run` writes only the report, only one run works on a checkout at a time, an interrupted run is finished or undone by the next run, and the report keeps the studies of earlier runs. The new **Study format 2 cutover** page explains the runbook.

- The local curation app takes the rules of the study format from the local server instead of its own copies (#877): the table files of a study with their kinds in workbook order, a preview of a new table and of the rows that a draft review target matches, the matched rows and the digitized series of each review target, digitized error bar ends as part of their series, suggestion kinds, and the review item of each change in the activity. Acknowledging a warning now covers exactly that warning: a warning without a row, such as `unknown_dataset` of a `.wpd.json` file or `review_target_unmatched`, is acknowledged by its key, the dataset name or the review item id, which `review.json` stores as the new `key` of a target, and `pkdb review acknowledge` and `pkdb review add` take `--key`. Acknowledgements of a whole file in existing `review.json` files keep their meaning, and the app says that they cover every warning of their code in that file. The app offers Acknowledge only for warnings of the validation, not for those of a sync that stopped before it. Validation messages of `study.json` lose pydantic's `Value error,` prefix, and `pkdb plot` lists the color of each series once.

- Curate study format 2 folders in a new local app, built into the `pkdb` package and opened by `pkdb curate` (#876). The overview lists the studies with their review status, problems, workbook sync and uploads; each study page has sections for the metadata of `study.json` with explicit saves that check the file on disk and merge your edits when it changed, the review items with their targets, the problems with acknowledgements, the sources with the paper image, the raw extraction and the mapped rows side by side and an interactive overlay of the WebPlotDigitizer points on the figure, the tables with their sync status and conflicts, and the activity with reports. An upload that `pkdb curate` was stopped during now stays an unknown outcome to check before uploading again, two `pkdb curate` instances keep separate sessions, and `pkdb plot` uses the same colorblind-safe colors as the app.

- The local curation app `pkdb curate` works on study format 2 folders only and keeps them in step on every save (#873). Studies are listed by `<substance>/<name>`; folders of study format 1 are counted but never changed, and their curation stays on the released app version until the migration. Each save syncs the workbook with the tables, formats them, validates and, with On save Upload, uploads; a conflict between the workbook and the tables stops until it is resolved, and closing a workbook that lacks table changes regenerates it. The GitHub issue of a study comes from the `issue` number in `study.json`. A local API serves study pages, tables, sources, images and the curator roster, and writes `study.json`, `review.json` and the workbook with revision checks; writes are signed by the PK-DB account of the API key or the configured user and are refused when the key belongs to another account, also for `pkdb study` and `pkdb review`, which now need `--user` or `PKDB_USER`.

- Keep the raw extraction of every paper table and figure beside the mapped rows, and edit `study.json` and `review.json` with checked `pkdb` commands (#872). A raw table `<study>_<source>.tsv` holds a paper table as printed: text cells that are never parsed or sorted, synced as a text sheet of the workbook and added with `pkdb tables add <study> --raw Tab2`. A figure keeps its WebPlotDigitizer project `<study>_<source>.wpd.json`, imported with `pkdb digitize import`; datasets named like a timecourse `label`, `<label>;error_bar` or a scatter `name` are compared with the rows, and `pkdb validate` warns about datasets without rows (`unknown_dataset`) and rows more than 2 pixels from their digitized point (`digitized_mismatch`). `pkdb plot` draws the digitized points and the rows on the figure image. `pkdb study show|patch|reference` and `pkdb review show|add|reply|resolve|dismiss|reopen|status|acknowledge` write `study.json` and `review.json` in canonical form and refuse to write over a file that changed since it was read; `--user`/`PKDB_USER` names the author and `--agent`/`PKDB_AGENT` the AI agent. `review.json` records who approved a study and when (`approved_by`, `approved`); approval requires zero open review items and zero validation errors and is refused for agents, and a dismissed review item no longer acknowledges a warning. The study folder names `outputs`, `timecourses` and `scatters` are reserved.

- Edit study format 2 tables in a generated Excel or LibreOffice workbook with `pkdb tables open`, `pkdb tables sync` and `pkdb tables add` (#870). The workbook `<name>.xlsx` is built from the TSV tables, which stay the only files to commit, with a frozen header with filters and column descriptions as comments, text-formatted columns and dropdowns for subjects, interventions and vocabulary terms; git should ignore `*.xlsx` and `.*.pkdb-base`, and `pkdb tables sync` and `pkdb tables open` warn when it does not or when it tracks them. A sync writes saved workbook edits to the tables in canonical form, updates the workbook with changes of the tables, and merges changes on both sides line by line. Conflicts list the workbook rows and table lines with both versions and are resolved with `--keep workbook` or `--keep tables`, or by editing the workbook, and `--check` reports without writing. A workbook that is open in a spreadsheet application is never replaced. Cells that the spreadsheet application converted (dates, percentages, error values, line breaks) and formulas without a saved value are reported at their cell, and formulas are stored as their values. `pkdb upload` syncs a study format 2 folder that has a workbook, then formats it, before preparing it, and stops on conflicts, whereas `pkdb validate` and `pkdb prepare` never write and report workbook changes that are not in the tables yet. Table file names longer than 31 characters and names that differ only in case are rejected, because Excel limits sheet names. See the new **Edit tables in a workbook** documentation page.

- Replace pandas with polars in the `pkdb` client (#864). Hidden TSV tables are read from study workbooks with calamine and tabulated with polars, about three times faster, and remain byte-identical to the earlier tables (8346 of 8347 tables in `pkdb_data`; one cell with a corrupted `_x005F_` escape decodes differently). R datasets are decoded into polars frames and record the polars version. A table line with more cells than its header now names the sheet and row. pandas is still installed indirectly through `pkpdutils` and the optional `rdata` reader.

- Upload study format 2 folders with `pkdb upload` and serve their studies by `<substance>/<name>` (#867), for example `/api/v2/studies/caffeine/Harder1988` and `/data/caffeine/Harder1988`. Studies report `pkdb_id`, `release_date`, `issue`, `review_status` and `open_review_items`. Uploading a released folder under a new location renames the stored study, a format 2 folder takes over the format 1 study of the same publication and source, and the PKDB identifier and the former format 1 identifier redirect (308) to the study. Statistics no longer have `value`: the value of one subject or of an unspecified summary is `mean`. The new `gmean`, `gsd`, `gcv`, `error_bar` and `error_type` are stored; `sd`, `se`, `cv`, `gsd` and `gcv` are derived from each other and from error bars, whereas `gmean` and `error_bar` are reported, not derived. Statistics that contradict each other by more than 2 percent and more than the rounding of their digits raise the warning `inconsistent_statistics`, measurement and intervention rows report their `count`, and pharmacokinetic parameters derived from a timecourse take its count. Dosing schedules are structured (`time` as a number or a list, `interval`, `doses`) instead of strings such as `0|12|40` or `S0T24R7`, which study format 1 sources still use. The web interface shows one compact Value column and the study status. Migration `p006studyformat` converts stored values and schedules, and processing version 9 requires every study to be uploaded again with a matching `pkdb` release. Reading the API needs the matching `pkdb` release as well, because older clients reject the new response fields, also with `--no-update` or `PKDB_NO_UPDATE`.
