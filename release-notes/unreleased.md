# Unreleased

- `pkdb migrate` writes a labelled array output whose label has a single row as an output instead of a timecourse of one point, which format 2 refuses (`timecourse_points`), such as the outputs of each individual at one time in tirzepatide/Urva2021 and apixaban/Frost2014. Its label is dropped and listed under Manual decisions of `migration.md`, like the labels of other array outputs that form no timecourse series (#968).
- The PK parameters of a timecourse are calculated when its dose has a unit with a numeric factor, such as `mg/(1.73*m^2)` for a dose per 1.73 m^2 body surface area. The calculation stopped with "Unit expression cannot have a scaling factor.", so `pkdb migrate` reported acetaminophen/Lowenthal1976 as `converter_error`, and `pkdb validate`, `pkdb check` and uploads failed the same way in both study formats. A dose per body surface area has no dose unit of the PK calculation, so the timecourse keeps its dose-independent parameters, and the factor of a dose unit such as `10^3*mg` scales its amount (#968).
