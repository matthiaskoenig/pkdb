# Unreleased

- `pkdb migrate` refuses a study with `unmapped_value` when a format 1 row that it converts holds a number in a column that `study.json` does not map, not only when it drops the row: the converter writes only the mapped columns, so the conversion lost the min, max or range next to a mapped median or mean, cv columns, digitized error bars whose spread is not mapped, baselines and changes, quartiles, the other dimension of a scatter and whole measurements that no template reads. An audit of the converted studies of pkdb_data found about 7,200 such numbers in 280 studies. The difference names the format 1 file, sheet and row, the subjects and measurements of its records and the columns with their numbers, such as `unmapped_value Frost2014.xlsx Fig2 row 3, subject all, concentration` with `value 16.87`. A number that a kept record of the row holds is no loss: a mapped column, a cell of the row that the formulas of mapped cells read (name formulas included, such as an index in the name of an individual), a cell that a formula computes only from held cells and counts or times, now also of other rows of the sheet (a change computed from the mean and the mean of the baseline row), and an exact copy of a statistic of the record or of the end of its error bar (a `value` column that repeats the mean, a `mean_pm` column that is the mean plus the typed sd). The columns `individual_count`, `subjects`, `day`, `tstart`, `tend`, `t_start`, `t_end`, `traw` and every column whose name starts with `time` hold no value. On the format 1 states of the 1448 converted studies the gate refuses 466 studies, where it refused 127 before; 430 of them lose numbers, 9 have error bar formulas that read the neighbouring row, and 27 only digitized helpers such as error bar ends next to a rounded spread or a logarithm column.
