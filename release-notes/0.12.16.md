# Release notes for pkdb 0.12.16

pkdb 0.12.16 extends the vocabulary for the review of the rapamycin (sirolimus) studies: new substances, ratios of pharmacokinetic parameters to a reference treatment, dose-normalized and trough measurements, blood cell counts, transplantation and other medical procedures, the harmonic mean and the smoking history. The database needs no migration; the server applies the new vocabulary at startup.

## Changes

- The vocabulary has the terms that the review of the rapamycin (sirolimus) studies needs (#1024):
    - substances `everolimus`, `rapamycin and metabolites` (total radioactivity), `prednisolone`, `corticosteroid` (an unnamed corticosteroid), `azathioprine`, `ombitasvir`, `paritaprevir`, `dasabuvir` and `calcium dobesilate`;
    - ratios of pharmacokinetic parameters to a reference treatment of the same subjects, such as the geometric mean ratios of interaction, food effect and bioequivalence studies: `cmax (ratio)`, `tmax (ratio)`, `thalf (ratio)`, `auc_inf (ratio)`, `auc_end (ratio)`, `oral clearance (ratio)`, `vd_ss (ratio)`, `mrt (ratio)`, `bp_ratio (ratio)`, `cmax_per_auc (ratio)`, the dose-normalized `cmax_per_dose (ratio)` and `auc_per_dose (ratio)`, and `concentration (ratio)` and `concentration per dose (ratio)` at a time;
    - the measurements `cmax_per_dose`, `cthrough_per_dose`, `dose_per_cthrough` and `cmax_per_auc`, and the blood cell counts `white blood cell count`, `neutrophil fraction` and `platelet count`;
    - the medical procedures `kidney transplantation`, `roux-en-y gastric bypass` and `liver biopsy`, the measurements `time since medical procedure` and `transplant donor` (`deceased donor`, `living donor`);
    - the calculation type `harmonic mean`, the ethnicity `non-black`, the `smoking history` (`never smoker`, `former smoker`, `current smoker`), the metabolic challenge `high-fat meal` and the ABCC2 genotypes `C3972T C/C`, `C3972T C/T` and `C3972T T/T`.
