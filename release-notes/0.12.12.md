# Release notes for pkdb 0.12.12

pkdb 0.12.12 converts more format 1 studies to study format 2: a paper table can have its raw table as its source when no image exists (unpublished or raw data), `pkdb migrate` keeps the WebPlotDigitizer projects of a format 1 folder, no longer refuses numbers that a kept record holds through a workbook formula, and an all-zero concentration curve no longer fails its study. The vocabulary has the measurements, units, substances, tissues, methods and choices that the remaining format 1 studies need. `pkdb plot`, the curation app and the website draw outputs by time only in figures whose x axis is time. The database needs no migration; the server applies the new vocabulary at startup, and `pkdb-server rebuild-views` builds the stored study pages again (their version changed).

## Changes

- A paper table of study format 2 needs its image `<study>_<source>.png` or its raw table `<study>_<source>.tsv` (#1012): the raw table is the source of data that no image shows, such as unpublished data, raw data provided elsewhere or a supplement that is not available, where before every source but `Text` needed its image (`missing_image`). A figure still needs its image. Rows of such a source have no image in PK-DB, the curation app and the website show the raw table and say that it is the source, and Add table and `pkdb tables add` do not ask for the image. `pkdb migrate` carries a raw table `<study>_<source>.tsv` that a curator put beside a format 1 workbook into the converted folder and converts its paper table without an image; the hidden format 1 tables `.<study>_<sheet>.tsv` are never raw tables.

- The plot of mapped rows of the local curation app and of the website, and `pkdb plot`, draw the rows of `outputs_<source>.tsv` with a `time` by time only when time is the x axis of the figure (#1011): when the figure has timecourse rows or an outputs series at two or more times. A figure whose every outputs series has one time, such as the change of HbA1c at the end of the treatment per exposure quartile of liraglutide/Ingwersen2012 Fig2, no longer has its rows drawn at the time on an axis of concentration or dose; its digitized points stay on the image, and `pkdb plot` no longer plots such a figure by default. A figure without rows to plot says **No rows over time or scatter rows to plot.** The stored study pages are built again (`VIEW_VERSION` 2, `pkdb-server rebuild-views`).

- `pkdb migrate` keeps the WebPlotDigitizer project `<study>_<source>.wpd.json` of a figure that a curator saved beside a format 1 workbook, where before it dropped it with the other JSON files (#1009). The project is checked against the figure image as `pkdb digitize import` checks it and written in canonical form; an invalid project, or one whose figure has no image, is dropped and listed under Manual decisions with the reason, and a kept project whose datasets differ from the converted rows is listed with its validation warnings.

- `pkdb migrate` no longer refuses a dropped row with `unmapped_value` for numbers that another record of the same row keeps through a formula of the workbook (#1010): a column that the formula of a mapped cell of that record reads, such as the `mean + se` column of an error bar `ABS(X - mean)`, and a column computed only from these and the count, such as an sd from that se. The C-peptide record of a format 1 row whose glucose was not sampled (`glucose/LicinioPaixao1986`) holds them, so the study converts.

- A concentration timecourse that is all zero, such as the concentrations of a placebo or below the limit of quantification, has no PK parameters, where before its study failed with `pk_zero_curve` (#1007). Three format 1 studies of pkdb_data that the conversion refused for it now convert.

- The vocabulary has the substances, tissues, methods and choices that format 1 studies need for their conversion to study format 2 (#1013): the substances `naltrexone` (morphine/Dayno2017), `mycophenolate mofetil` and `prednisone` (rapamycin/Rogers2008), `bupropion hydrochloride` (chlorzoxazone/Puris2019), `black grape juice` (omeprazole/Iftikhar2024), the protein `CYP2E1` (chlorzoxazone/Mishin1998) and the transporters `ABCB1`, `ABCC2`, `ABCC3`, `ABCG2`, `SLC10A2`, `SLC15A1`, `SLC22A1`, `SLC22A3` and `SLCO2B1` (named by their genes, with the protein names such as P-gp, BCRP, ASBT, PEPT1, OCT1 and OATP2B1 as synonyms, talinolol/Drozdzik2014); the tissues `duodenum`, `jejunum`, `ileum`, `colon`, `ascending colon`, `transverse colon`, `descending colon` and `sigmoid colon`; the methods `Jaffe reaction`, `Jaffe reaction (AutoAnalyzer)` and `Jaffe reaction (Lloyd reagent)` (the regular, the AutoAnalyzer and the true creatinine of creatinine/Bauer1982), `qRT-PCR`, `western blot`, `intra-arterial catheter`, `pulmonary artery catheter` and `thermodilution` (ramipril/deGraeff1989); the ethnicity `other` (semaglutide/Buckley2018), the disease `hypertriglyceridemia` and the medical procedure `lipoprotein apheresis` (pravastatin/Raasch1991).

- The vocabulary has the measurements that format 1 studies need for their conversion to study format 2 (#1013): `urinary volume rate (change)`, `excretion rate (change)` and `osmolarity (change)` (changes from baseline per collection interval, as in canagliflozin/Iijima2015), `auc_net_incremental` (the net area of a curve above minus below its baseline, such as the decremental AUC of blood glucose, time required) and `concentration change absolute maximum` and `concentration change absolute minimum` (the maximum increase and decrease from baseline, glimepiride/Niemi2002), `radioactivity concentration` (Bq/l or a count rate in cpm/ml) and `arteriovenous concentration difference` (inulin/Cousins1999), `mRNA expression` (relative to a reference gene of the same sample) and `protein amount` (pmol/mg protein by targeted proteomics or arbitrary units per mg protein by western blot, talinolol/Drozdzik2014 and chlorzoxazone/Mishin1998), and `filtered load` (such as the filtered glucose, empagliflozin/Sarashina2014). All changes, the net incremental AUC and the arteriovenous difference may be negative. The unit `cpm` (counts per minute) has its own dimension, so a count rate never converts to an activity in Bq.

- The web interface guide describes the Studies page and the study page with what each reader sees, and the local curation guide notes that the website shows a study with the same views; all screenshots of both guides are rendered again, the website ones with the new `tools/website_docs/run.sh` on the disposable website of the browser tests. (#963, #1008)
