# Release notes for pkdb 0.12.6

pkdb 0.12.6 adds the vocabulary terms that the format 1 studies of pkdb_data need for their conversion to study format 2 and fixes the units of some existing terms. `pkdb migrate` writes labelled array outputs with a single row per label as outputs instead of timecourses of one point, and the PK parameters of a timecourse whose dose unit has a numeric factor, such as `mg/(1.73*m^2)`, no longer stop validation, conversion and upload. The database needs no migration; the server applies the new vocabulary at startup.

## Changes

- The vocabulary has the terms that the format 1 studies of pkdb_data use but lacked (#969): 37 measurements, among them compartmental PK parameters (`k10`, `k12`, `k21`, `kdist`, `thalf_distribution`, `vd_peripheral`, `clearance_intercompartmental`, `clearance_distribution`, `auc_incremental`, `auc_extrapolated`), ratios to baseline such as `kel (ratio)` and `MAP (ratio)`, coagulation measurements such as `quick value`, `platelet aggregation` and `bleeding time` with their relative changes, and cardiovascular measurements such as `right atrial pressure`, `artery diameter`, `artery blood flow`, `blood velocity` and `vascular resistance`; 17 substances, such as erlotinib, OSI-420, levothyroxine, vinorelbine, fosinopril, insulin icodec, prothrombin complex concentrate, sorafenib-glucuronide and the morphine glucuronide ratios `m3g/mor` and `m6g/mor`; the methods ECLIA, IRMA, LOCI and coulometry; the tissue `brachial artery`; the SLCO1B3 genotype and the ABCB1 choices `CGC/CGC` and `TTT/TTT`; and the units `ml/kg` for dosing, `IU/l` for ACE activity, `1/min/kg` for kel, `ml/m^2` for heart volume and `g/kg` for tissue concentrations. Existing terms got corrected units: vascular resistance `dyn*s/cm^5` instead of a unit of another dimension, pulmonary capillary wedge pressure and pulmonary arterial pressure mmHg, `ETP` an amount concentration times time, `TGP` a concentration and `ETP (change relative)` percent; diethyldithiocarbamic acid has its correct formula and mass. PK parameters are derived only from concentrations per volume, so a tissue concentration timecourse in `g/kg` keeps its values without a Cmax or AUC.

- `pkdb migrate` writes a labelled array output whose label has a single row as an output instead of a timecourse of one point, which format 2 refuses (`timecourse_points`), such as the outputs of each individual at one time in tirzepatide/Urva2021 and apixaban/Frost2014 (#968). Its label is dropped and listed under Manual decisions of `migration.md`, like the labels of other array outputs that form no timecourse series.

- The PK parameters of a timecourse are calculated when its dose has a unit with a numeric factor, such as `mg/(1.73*m^2)` for a dose per 1.73 m^2 body surface area (#968). The calculation stopped with "Unit expression cannot have a scaling factor.", so `pkdb migrate` reported acetaminophen/Lowenthal1976 as `converter_error`, and `pkdb validate`, `pkdb check` and uploads failed the same way in both study formats. A dose per body surface area has no dose unit of the PK calculation, so the timecourse keeps its dose-independent parameters, and the factor of a dose unit such as `10^3*mg` scales its amount.

- The source files of a study are listed in the same order on Windows as on Linux and macOS (#957). Windows paths sorted without regard to case, so the files of a study row of the local curation app came in another order, and the contract test of the rows failed in the Windows client tests of the release run of 0.12.5.
