# Release notes for pkdb 0.12.14

pkdb 0.12.14 extends the vocabulary for hydrochlorothiazide/Ernst2006: the substance `chlorthalidone` and the method `ambulatory blood pressure monitoring`. The database needs no migration; the server applies the new vocabulary at startup.

## Changes

- The vocabulary has the substance `chlorthalidone` (chlortalidone, CHEBI:3654), the thiazide-like diuretic that hydrochlorothiazide/Ernst2006 compares with hydrochlorothiazide, and the method `ambulatory blood pressure monitoring` (ABPM), the repeated readings of a portable automatic cuff device over 24 hours whose 24-hour, daytime, nighttime or hourly means studies report, as distinct from the office measurement with a `sphygmomanometer` (#1019).
