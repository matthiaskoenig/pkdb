# Release notes for pkdb 0.12.3

pkdb 0.12.3 makes the local curation app ready for the curation of the converted pkdb_data studies and labels the issue of a study with every substance it has data of. The overview filters the studies by curator, with Me first, and keeps its filters when a study is opened; the Tables section hides empty columns; workbooks and the app put the characteristica after the subjects. `pkdb issues sync` adds a label for every other substance folder that the interventions and the data of a study name; run it with this release, because older releases remove these labels again. The client gives idle connections up before the server closes them, and `pkdb upload` of a parent folder uploads the other studies when some share an identity. The database needs no migration.

## Changes

- The characteristica follow the subjects: generated workbooks order their sheets `subjects`, `characteristica`, `interventions`, then the outputs, timecourses, scatters and raw tables, and the local curation app shows the tabs of the Tables section, the mapped rows of a source and the notes per table in the same order (#912). The characteristica describe the subjects, so they belong next to them. A workbook keeps its sheet order until it is generated again, as after a change of the tables or after it was deleted; `study.json` and the tables are not changed.

- `pkdb issues sync` labels the issue of a study with every substance of the repository that the study has data of, not only with the substance of its folder (#911). Besides the folder substance, each substance folder that the study names in its interventions, outputs, timecourses or scatters becomes a label, so that a study of talinolol whose subjects also got midazolam is found under `midazolam` too; before, only the folder substance was a label. In pkdb_data, 130 of the 849 study format 2 studies get further labels, most often glucose, morphine, midazolam and creatinine.

- The overview of the local curation app shows the names and pictures of the curators after a launch URL was pasted into an open overview without a session (#910). Before, it kept their initials and the Curator filter their user names until the page was reloaded, because it loaded the curator roster only when it opened.

- The search and the selects of the overview stay in place when the counts of the status chips change (#910). Before, the search field took the width that the chips left, so it grew or shrank by a few pixels with each filter, and a second line of chips moved every field down.

- The overview of the local curation app filters the studies by curator (#908). **Curator** lists every curator of the workspace with the number of their studies, and **Me**, the PK-DB user of the app, first, so a curator sees their own studies. The filters and the sort order of the overview stay when a study is opened and the overview is shown again; before, every visit of a study reset them.

- The Tables section of the local curation app hides the empty columns when it opens, because most tables leave many of their fixed columns empty (#907). The switch **Hide empty columns** shows them again.

- `pkdb curate` without the built app names the folder where it looked and the source checkout that runs (#906). Before, it only said `The curation app is not built. Run npm ci and npm run build:curation in frontend/.`, so a user with an installed release and an active virtual environment of a pkdb checkout could not tell that the checkout ran instead of the release. The guide of the local curation app no longer says that `pkdb curate` is missing from the releases; it is part of `pkdb` since 0.12.1.

- The client gives an idle connection to PK-DB up after two seconds instead of five (#905). uvicorn closes a connection after five idle seconds, so a write sent on a connection that it closed at that moment got no answer, ended as `unreachable` with an unknown outcome and stopped the batch; this happened once in an upload of all pkdb_data studies with eight jobs.

- `pkdb upload` of a parent folder uploads the other studies when some of them share a study identifier or a publication and source (#904). Before, one such pair, such as two pkdb_data folders whose `study.json` names the same PubMed ID, stopped the whole batch with `Multiple studies claim reference ...` before any upload; now each study of the pair fails with `identity_conflict` and names the others, because PK-DB keeps one study per publication and source and the order of the uploads would decide which one.
