# Release notes for pkdb 0.12.11

pkdb 0.12.11 shows the study format 2 studies on the website with the views of the local curation app, and no longer warns about the rows of dismissed review items, which pkdb_data needs for review items that no longer apply. The database needs no migration.

## Changes

- A dismissed review item no longer warns `review_target_unmatched` when the rows it targets change or go away, such as the acknowledgement of a duplicate observation whose rows got their own subjects (#1005): it no longer applies, and no command can retarget it. Open and resolved items still warn.

- The website lists the study format 2 studies on a new Studies page, with the search and the Substance and Curator filters of the local curation app under the website's own heading, and shows a study at `/data/<substance>/<name>` with the views of the curation app, in its compact sizes (#963, #1003): Metadata and Data for everyone, and for who may write the study also Problems, Review, the issue, the review status, the PDF and the figure images and raw tables. The page follows the session, keeps the data search that opened it in its address, and studies without a stored view, such as study format 1 studies and study format 2 studies uploaded before this release, keep the record page.

- A browser that still holds the cookie of an ended session (expired, revoked or signed out elsewhere) no longer makes the website read again without end (#1003): the first read deletes the cookie and the reader continues as a visitor.

- The website now declares the order of Vuetify's style layers before any stylesheet loads, so buttons on every page show Vuetify's size and weight instead of the size of the text around them (#1003).
