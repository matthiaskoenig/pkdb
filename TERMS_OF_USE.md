# PK-DB Terms of Use

The maintained [PK-DB Terms of Use](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/pkdb/terms-of-use/) are part of the documentation. Read the [documentation source](docs/terms-of-use.md) in this checkout.

The terms cover required citation of the PK-DB paper and original study publications, coauthorship for substantial reuse of more than 10 studies, and licensing for commercial use. Contact [Matthias König](mailto:koenigmx@hu-berlin.de) for collaboration and licensing.
